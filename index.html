    Privacy

    Your games, your data, your PC

    Lobby has no server. It reads what your launchers already know and keeps everything else on your machine.

    Your passwords stay with the storesYou sign in on their official pages. Lobby only keeps a token, in the Windows Credential Manager.

    Nothing is injected into your gamesNo overlay, no hooks: no trouble with anti-cheat, no FPS cost.

    Light and nativeWritten in Rust with Tauri: a few MB, and it stays out of the way in the system tray.

    FAQ

    Frequently asked questions

    Does Lobby replace Steam or Epic?
    No: your games stay in their stores, which keep handling downloads and updates. Lobby brings them together so you no longer have to open each launcher.
    Is it really free?
    Yes, with no ads and no subscription. A few extras (deals, alternative covers, game length) use free services you get your own key for in two minutes.
    How is play time counted?
    Every 10 seconds, Lobby looks at which programs run from one of your games' folders, like Task Manager does. It takes a few milliseconds, and you can turn it off in Settings, for all games or just one.
    What about games that don't come from a store?
    Add them by hand: an .exe, a .bat or a .cmd, with arguments if needed. Lobby finds cover art for them, or you pick your own.
    Windows says "Windows protected your PC". Is it safe?
    Lobby's installer isn't code-signed yet (a certificate costs several hundred euros a year), so SmartScreen doesn't know it. Click "More info", then "Run anyway". Every release is built from the same code and published on GitHub.
    Does it run on Mac or Linux?
    Not for now: Lobby is made for Windows, where the launchers it brings together live.
